# Invoice changelog

## 1.4 - 2026-11-26

- Tax documents and final invoices created from proforma invoices can be saved again: the **Invoice Type** field offers **Tax Document** and **Final Invoice**.

## 1.3 - 2026-10-26

- The **Received** field can now be edited in the invoice form, in mass edit and inline in the detail view.
- The **Balance** field is calculated automatically as Grand Total minus Received every time the invoice is saved. It is shown in the detail view and the list, and is also calculated on newly installed systems.

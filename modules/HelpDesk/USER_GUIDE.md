# Tickets

Tickets help support teams record customer requests, track their status and priority, and keep the related communication together.

## Converting a ticket to the knowledge base

Open a ticket and choose **More → Convert to Knowledge Base** to prepare an FAQ article. The new article starts as a draft and uses the ticket subject, solution, and public comments as its initial content.

Internal comments are for CRM users only and are not copied to the FAQ article. Review the draft before publishing it to the customer portal.

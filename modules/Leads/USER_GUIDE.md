# Leads – User Guide

Leads are people or companies that may become customers but have not yet been qualified. Use this module to collect initial information, qualify interest, and decide whether the lead should become a proper customer record.

## What you can do

- Store the lead's name, company, title, contact details, website, industry, and source.
- Assign the lead to a responsible user.
- Track the lead with a status such as **Not Contacted**, **Attempted to Contact**, **Contacted**, **Contact in Future**, **Cold**, **Warm**, **Hot**, **Pre Qualified**, **Qualified**, **Lost Lead**, or **Junk Lead**.
- Relate products, services, campaigns, emails, appointments, documents, and comments to the lead.
- Use the list view, filters, and lead-status charts to organise follow-up work.
- Convert a qualified lead into an Opportunity, with a Contact and, when you enter an organization name, an Organization.

## Typical workflow

1. Create a lead from an enquiry, campaign, referral, import, or another source.
2. Complete the available contact and company information and select a **Lead Source** and **Lead Status**.
3. Assign the lead to the person responsible for follow-up.
4. Record calls, meetings, emails, notes, and other follow-up in the related sections.
5. Update the status as the lead is contacted and qualified.
6. Use **Convert Lead** when the lead becomes a real sales relationship.

## Converting a lead

**Convert Lead** (available on the lead's detail view to users with the Convert Lead permission) opens a window with the values for the new records. A Contact and an Opportunity are always created. An Organization is created only when you enter an organization name; if an organization with that name already exists, it is reused instead of duplicated. The values are pre-filled from the lead according to **Settings > Module Manager > Leads > Leads Field Mapping**, so review them before confirming.

On conversion, the lead's documents, products, related records, campaigns and comments are moved to the Opportunity, and its appointments are linked to the new Organization, Contact and Opportunity. The Contact is also related to the Opportunity. The lead is then marked as converted and no longer appears in the lead list or lead dashboards. You are taken to the new Opportunity. A lead that is already converted cannot be converted again.

## Helpful tips

- Use **Lead Source** consistently so you can compare campaigns and acquisition channels.
- Keep the status current; it is the quickest way to separate new, active, qualified, and unusable leads.
- Check for an existing contact before converting, because conversion always creates a new Contact. If duplicate checking finds a matching record, the conversion stops with an explanatory message.
- Add a clear next activity before leaving a lead in an active status.

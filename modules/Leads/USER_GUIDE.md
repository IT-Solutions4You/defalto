# Leads – User Guide

Leads are people or companies that may become customers but have not yet been qualified. Use this module to collect initial information, qualify interest, and decide whether the lead should become a proper customer record.

## What you can do

- Store the lead's name, company, title, contact details, website, industry, and source.
- Assign the lead to a responsible user.
- Track the lead with a status such as **Not Contacted**, **Contacted**, **Pre Qualified**, **Qualified**, **Hot**, **Warm**, **Cold**, **Contact in Future**, **Junk Lead**, or **Lost Lead**.
- Relate products, services, campaigns, emails, activities, documents, and comments to the lead.
- Use the list view, filters, and lead-status charts to organise follow-up work.
- Convert a qualified lead into an Organization, Contact, and optionally an Opportunity.

## Typical workflow

1. Create a lead from an enquiry, campaign, referral, import, or another source.
2. Complete the available contact and company information and select a **Lead Source** and **Lead Status**.
3. Assign the lead to the person responsible for follow-up.
4. Record calls, meetings, emails, notes, and other follow-up activities in the related sections.
5. Update the status as the lead is contacted and qualified.
6. Use **Convert Lead** when the lead becomes a real sales relationship.

## Converting a lead

Conversion creates or selects the customer records that will be used from that point forward. Depending on the information and permissions available, you can create or select an **Organization** and **Contact**, and create an **Opportunity** for the sales deal. Products and services related to the lead can be carried into the converted records when supported by the conversion mapping.

Review the proposed values before confirming the conversion. After conversion, the lead is no longer part of the active lead list and its history remains available through the resulting records.

## Helpful tips

- Use **Lead Source** consistently so you can compare campaigns and acquisition channels.
- Keep the status current; it is the quickest way to separate new, active, qualified, and unusable leads.
- Do not create duplicate customer records during conversion. Select an existing organization or contact when one already exists.
- Add a clear next activity before leaving a lead in an active status.

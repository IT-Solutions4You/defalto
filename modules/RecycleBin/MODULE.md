# RecycleBin

- `RecycleBin_ListView_Model` owns deleted-record lists and counts. Its factory builds a `Core_QueryGenerator_Model` for the source module on its "All" filter; `parseAdvFilterList()` wraps OR groups inside the surrounding constraints. List and count both take the normal query and rewrite `vtiger_crmentity.deleted = 0` to `deleted = 1`; keep that rewrite and the source-module permissions in both paths.
- `RecycleBinAjax` owns the actions. Restore and permanent delete need the `Delete` permission on the source module; `emptyRecycleBin` is administrator-only. The source-module list excludes ProjectMilestone, ModComments, Integration, PBXManager, Dashboard, Home and non-entity modules.
- Permanent deletion (`RecycleBin_Module_Model::deleteRecords()` and `emptyRecycleBin()`) removes the `vtiger_relatedlists_rb` and `vtiger_modtracker_basic` rows and the `vtiger_crmentity` rows of deleted records. `deleteRecords()` also deletes the related ModComments. `deleteFiles()` removes the `vtiger_seattachmentsrel` and `vtiger_attachments` rows and unlinks the physical files, resolving `vtiger_attachments.path` through `Core_Storage_Model`, so legacy relative and configured absolute storage paths both work.
- `RecycleBin_Install_Model` registers a standard module without owned tables.
- Validate filtered list/count consistency, exclusion of active records and attachment cleanup; lint touched PHP files.

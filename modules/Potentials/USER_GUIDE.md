# Opportunities – User Guide

Opportunities represent active sales deals that have progressed beyond an initial lead. Use them to track the customer, expected value, sales stage, probability, next action, and expected closing date.

The module is also the centre of the sales pipeline. An opportunity can be linked to an organization, contact, products, services, quotes, sales orders, invoices, projects, appointments, emails, and documents.

## What you can do

- Track each deal through the sales funnel.
- Record the expected amount, currency, probability, and expected close date.
- Keep the next step visible for the person responsible for the deal.
- Review related contacts, products, services, quotes, sales orders, and invoices from the opportunity.
- Open the **Kanban View** from the list view to review opportunities by sales stage.
- Create a Quote, Sales Order, or Invoice directly from the opportunity when the relevant information is ready (see Related module actions).
- Create a Project from an opportunity with **Create Project** (shown until the opportunity has been converted and only if you may create Projects).
- Review the **Sales Stage History** and use reports or dashboards for pipeline and forecast monitoring.

## Typical workflow

1. Create an opportunity and link it to the customer organization and, when useful, a contact.
2. Enter the expected amount, currency, expected close date, sales stage, probability, and next step.
3. Add the products or services being discussed.
4. Move the sales stage forward as the deal progresses.
5. Create a Quote, Sales Order, or Invoice from the opportunity when appropriate.
6. Finish the opportunity as **Closed Won** or **Closed Lost** and keep the reason or notes in the record.

## Sales stages

The standard stages describe the customer's buying process: **Prospecting**, **Qualification**, **Needs Analysis**, **Value Proposition**, **Identify Decision Makers**, **Perception Analysis**, **Proposal or Price Quote**, **Negotiation/Review**, **Closed Won**, and **Closed Lost**. Your installation may contain additional or renamed picklist values.

The **Probability** and **Weighted Revenue** help with forecasting. Weighted Revenue is calculated automatically on every save as the opportunity amount multiplied by its probability (in percent), in the opportunity's currency, so keep both the amount and probability current when the deal changes.

## Helpful tips

- Keep **Next Step** specific, for example “Send revised quote on Friday”.
- Use the related lists to keep the commercial history in one place instead of creating disconnected records.
- Check the **Expected Close Date** before pipeline reviews so forecasts use realistic dates.
- Use **Closed Lost** rather than deleting a lost deal; this preserves pipeline history.

## Related module actions

From an opportunity, users with the required permissions can create a Quote, Sales Order, or Invoice (**Create Quote**, **Create Sales Order**, **Create Invoice** in the **More** menu). The new record is linked back to the opportunity so the sales history remains traceable.

# Products

Products maintains the product catalog used by related CRM records and inventory documents. Open a product to review its details and related lists, such as price books, product bundles (with the bundle's total cost), Quotes, Sales Orders, Invoices, Purchase Orders, opportunities, documents and appointments.

## Quick preview

Use the eye icon to inspect a product without leaving the current screen. Available actions follow your record permissions. Delete requires confirmation; cancellation or an error leaves the preview open. After a successful deletion the preview closes and the source screen refreshes. Close and reopen the preview to inspect another product.

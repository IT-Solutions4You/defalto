# Products

Products maintains the inventory catalog used by related CRM records and inventory documents. Open a product to review its details and supported related lists, including price books and product bundles.

Use the eye icon to inspect a product without leaving the current screen. Available actions follow your record permissions. Delete requires confirmation; cancellation or an error leaves the preview open. After successful deletion the preview closes and the source screen refreshes. Close and reopen the preview to inspect another product.

# CRM Settings

## Company details

Administrators maintain the company name, contact and bank information, description and the logo shown throughout the CRM in Settings, under Company Details.

### Company logo

- Upload a JPEG, PNG or GIF image no larger than the maximum upload size configured in the CRM and no bigger than 5000 x 5000 pixels.
- The file extension must match the image's real format. For example, a PNG renamed to `.jpg` is rejected with an explanation.
- If an upload is not accepted, the form stays open and an error notification gives the reason.
- To replace the logo, choose a new image and save the company details.
- To remove the logo, edit the company details, click the remove button on the logo preview and save. The button appears only for a custom logo.
- Without a custom logo, the CRM shows the default Defalto logo.

## Summary widgets

Administrators can arrange the summary widgets shown on each module's record detail pages in Settings, under Widget editor in the Module Manager section. Widgets sit in a left or right column and can be reordered, disabled or, for custom list widgets, removed.

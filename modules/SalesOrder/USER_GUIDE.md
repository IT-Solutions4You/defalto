# Sales Orders – User Guide

Sales Orders record what the customer has agreed to buy and provide the operational hand-off after a quote is accepted. A Sales Order can be linked to an organization, contact, opportunity, and quote and contains the confirmed products or services.

## What you can do

- Create a Sales Order directly or generate it from a Quote or Opportunity.
- Confirm products or services, quantities, pricing, discounts, taxes, and totals.
- Record the order date, due date, customer reference, purchase order number, tracking number, and terms.
- Track order progress with statuses such as **Created**, **Approved**, **Delivered**, or **Cancelled**.
- Export the order to PDF or send it by email with the PDF attached.
- Create an Invoice from the Sales Order.
- Configure recurring invoicing with a frequency, start period, end period, payment duration, and next invoice date when the order represents a repeating service.

## Typical workflow

1. Create a Sales Order from the accepted quote, opportunity, or Sales Orders module.
2. Verify the customer, contact, source quote, order date, due date, and customer references.
3. Review every line item and the financial summary.
4. Save the order and update its status as it is approved and delivered.
5. Create an Invoice when the order should be billed.
6. For recurring services, enable recurring invoicing and verify the schedule before saving.

## Recurring invoicing

Recurring invoicing is useful for subscriptions, maintenance, and other repeating services. Select the frequency and define the start and end periods, payment duration, and next invoice date. Review the schedule whenever the order period or billing arrangement changes.

## Helpful tips

- Treat an approved Sales Order as the agreed commercial instruction for fulfilment and billing.
- Keep the related Quote selected so users can compare the order with the accepted offer.
- Use **Delivered** only when the delivery or service milestone has actually been completed.
- Check whether the order should create a regular or recurring invoice before creating the first invoice.
